# Voice

Plain, specific, a person who makes things talking to a person who buys
them. Sentences a buyer can check against the photos.

- Lead with the object and what is particular about it, not with adjectives.
  "A drink coaster built the way kumiko woodwork is" beats "Stunning unique
  coaster".
- One idea per sentence. No exclamation marks, no emoji in the Etsy text.
  An Instagram caption may carry one emoji at most, and does not need one.
- Say what it is made of and how: 3D printed, to order, in PLA, a
  plant-based plastic. Say that fine layer lines are part of how it is made.
- Sizes in both systems: "10 cm (3.9 in)".
- Care is honest: wipe clean, keep away from heat above a hot drink, not for
  the dishwasher.
- Never "handmade" for the object itself; it is printed. "Made to order" is
  true and is the phrase to use.
- British or American spelling: follow the comparables' titles, because that
  is what buyers type; do not mix the two in one listing.
